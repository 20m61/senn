# SENN Trademark Policy

The Apache-2.0 license grants broad rights to use, modify, and redistribute the
SENN source code. This trademark policy describes the separate rules that apply
to the **SENN** name, logo, and related marks.

## Marks Covered

- **SENN**
- **SENN Core**
- **SENN Add-ons**
- **SENN Registry**
- **SENN Verified**

(Hereafter, the "Marks".)

## Permitted Use

You may use the Marks to:

- Refer to the SENN project and its software factually
  (e.g. "an add-on for SENN").
- State that your software is compatible with SENN, provided the statement is
  accurate and not misleading.
- Use unmodified screenshots and logos in articles, talks, and tutorials about
  SENN, as long as attribution is preserved.

## Restricted Use

Without prior written permission from the SENN steering committee, you may not:

- Use the Marks in the name of a product, service, company, or domain in a way
  that suggests official affiliation, endorsement, or origin.
- Use **SENN Verified** to describe your distribution unless the artifact has
  actually been signed by the official SENN Verified Registry.
- Modify the SENN logo, or use a confusingly similar logo.
- Use the Marks for projects that fork SENN with substantive changes, unless
  the fork is clearly identified as a fork and not as official SENN.

## Enforcement

The steering committee will, where possible, contact apparent violators in
good faith before pursuing other remedies. The intent of this policy is to keep
"SENN" meaningful for users — not to restrict legitimate community use.

## Questions

Open an issue or contact the steering committee.
